What Is Nashite Fukuoka's Autumn Utage?

Our workshops have introduced us to so many fascinating people.

But each session draws a different crowd — many of you still haven't met each other.

The Autumn Utage is a day to make that connection happen.

This isn't a business networking event. Work, hobbies, play, everyday life — it's all welcome here. A place for people to slow down and really meet each other.

Why It's Okay to Come Alone

“A room full of strangers feels a little daunting…”
If that's you, here's something worth knowing.

On the day, our core team plays matchmaker and works the room.

“Oh, you're into ___? There's someone over there who loves that too.”
“If that's your thing, you have to talk to them — it'll be great.”

We never let anyone stand alone. That's the Nashite Fukuoka way.

Even the name tags have a little trick: alongside your name, you'll add what you love, what you're into right now, or what's on your mind. You'll probably never run out of conversation starters.

3 Highlights of the Day

01

Mic Pitch

Three minutes, one mic. Introduce yourself, share what you're working on, or what you really want to do — it's your time to speak freely. Volunteers welcome.

02

Multicultural Feast

Members who've traveled the world as nomads — and love to cook — recreate dishes from different countries. You might find dishes you won't find anywhere else in Fukuoka.

03

Icebreakers for First-Timers

Mini games and quizzes help strangers warm up naturally. We also mix up the groups so people don't just stick with who they already know.
